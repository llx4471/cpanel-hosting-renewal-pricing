## Why cheap cPanel hosting prices differ by a factor of ten

The license is the reason. cPanel charges per account, and hosts handle that bill in three different ways.

**Bundled into a shared plan.** RackNerd includes cPanel in a plan that starts at $5.59/mo, alongside LiteSpeed and CloudLinux. At HostFinch's à la carte rates, LiteSpeed alone runs $38.99/mo for 1 CPU and $88.99/mo for 8 CPUs, CloudLinux is $16.99/mo, and a single-account cPanel license is $26.00/mo. On shared hosting those are the host's costs, not yours.

**Priced as a separate line item on a VPS.** HostFinch sells cPanel hosting from $23.60/mo for its Solo plan (bare metal $24.54/mo) and $61.36/mo for Premium with branding and extra accounts, and it also publishes the raw license rates: $26.00/mo for one account, $66.00/mo for 100.

**Sold as a VPS plus a license you buy yourself.** Hostinger's cPanel VPS starts at $6.49/mo for KVM 1 (1 vCPU, 4 GB RAM, 50 GB NVMe, 4 TB bandwidth), but the license is not in that price. You pick the AlmaLinux 8 with cPanel template, then purchase a control panel license separately, with a higher tier if you want to resell accounts. Hostinger also states plainly that it doesn't provide managed VPS hosting, so server administration is yours.

Add those up: a $6.49 VPS plus a $26 license lands near $32/mo before you've configured anything. That's what "cheap cPanel hosting" means when the license sits outside the plan.

Pick by who fixes the server at 2am.

## FAQ

**Can I bring my own cPanel license to a cheap VPS?**
Sometimes, but the license is tied to the server and the host's terms decide. Hostinger's flow is to buy the license through the control panel after installing the cPanel template. If license portability matters, confirm it with the host's sales team before ordering.

**How many websites fit on the cheapest cPanel plan?**
On RackNerd's 30 GB tier, 2 domains. The 85 GB tier allows 10, and the 200 GB tier is unlimited. Databases are unlimited on all three.

**Will my existing cPanel sites migrate?**
Yes, if your current host runs cPanel. RackNerd includes free migration from cPanel providers, and cPanel accounts transfer with files, databases and email intact.

**Do I need to buy LiteSpeed or CloudLinux separately?**
Not on RackNerd shared hosting; both are part of the stack. On a VPS they're paid add-ons, and at HostFinch's rates LiteSpeed runs $38.99–$88.99/mo and CloudLinux $16.99/mo, which can exceed the cost of the server itself.

**Can I resell cPanel accounts?**
Yes, through the reseller line, which includes WHM, whitelabel nameservers and a free Clientexec license, with additional cPanel accounts at $0.50/mo or $6.00/yr. Buying a higher-tier cPanel license on an unmanaged VPS is possible at some hosts, including Hostinger, but you'd still be administering the server.
